<Info>
本页是**大作文-图像**：重点解决单幅漫画、双幅漫画和对比图画的第一段描述，第二、三段继续使用 F3—F5 通用框架。
</Info>

## 1. 总览与考场速查

<Info>
图画作文固定使用三段五功能：F1 描述画面，F2 提炼关键细节、对比或寓意，F3 写意义或原因，F4 总体评论，题干要求时再写 F5 建议或展望。
</Info>

### 1.1 图画作文五功能

| 功能 | 图画类任务 |
| --- | --- |
| F1 | 描述人物、动作、场景或双幅对比 |
| F2 | 提炼关键细节、对比或中心寓意 |
| F3 | 说明寓意、现实影响或可能原因 |
| F4 | 给出总体评价或明确立场 |
| F5 | 按题目要求提出建议或展望 |

### 1.2 按题目指令决定功能

| 题目要求 | 应写功能 |
| --- | --- |
| 只要求描述图画 | F1 + F2 |
| 要求描述并解释 | F1 + F2 + F3 |
| 要求描述、解释并评论 | F1 + F2 + F3 + F4 |
| 评论中还要求建议或未来 | F1 + F2 + F3 + F4 + F5 |

F5 不是固定结尾。图画题不要强行加入社会原因，也不要把普通动作无限拔高。

### 1.3 推荐篇幅

- 第一段：2—3 句。
- 第二段：2—3 句。
- 第三段：2—3 句。

### 1.4 图画题从审题到成文

1. 判断是单幅图还是双幅图。
2. 写 F1，只描述与寓意有关的人物、动作和对比。
3. 写 F2，用一个关键细节或一组对比推出寓意。
4. 写 F3，优先走意义路径，必要时再解释原因。
5. 题目要求评论时写 F4；要求建议或未来时再写 F5。
6. 检查时态、人物指代和寓意是否过度拔高。

## 2. 第一段：图画呈现

### F1 描述画面

**表达目的**

先写清画面中的人物、动作、场景和对比关系，不提前写寓意。寓意留给 F2。

**核心英文句型**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="单幅图">
    > The picture shows how `[person]` chooses to `[behavior]` in `[context]`.

    > The picture presents a scene in which `[person]` is `[doing something]`, while `[another person]` is `[doing something else]`.

    > In the picture, `[person]` can be seen `[doing something]`, even though `[difficulty]` makes the situation challenging.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="双幅图">
    > While the first picture shows `[A]`, the second shows `[B]`, which creates a clear contrast.

    > The two pictures both focus on `[theme]`, but they present it from different angles.

    > The two pictures form a sharp contrast: `[A]`, whereas `[B]`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**填空与限制**

- 单幅图写人物、动作和场景；双幅图先写共同主题或对比关系。
- 用一般现在时描述画面。
- 不要把 `The picture illustrates the importance of...` 当作 F1，它属于寓意或评论。
- 不要描写与主题无关的背景、天气或衣着。

### F2 提取重点并概括

**中文结构**

`关键动作或细节 + 画面寓意`，或 `双幅对比 + 共同寓意`。

**核心英文句型**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="单幅图">
    > What is most striking is that `[detail]`, which suggests `[meaning]`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="双幅图">
    > The key contrast is between `[A]` and `[B]`: `[A]` `[action or state]`, whereas `[B]` `[opposite action or state]`, which suggests `[meaning]`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**使用规则**

- 只写一个最关键的细节或一组最鲜明的对比。
- 寓意必须能从画面细节自然推出，不能直接跳到大词。
- 图画题不一定需要原因分析；强调寓意时优先走 F3 意义路径。
- 不要让 F1 和 F2 重复同一句。


**从句扩展：画面描述**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="用 while / which / how 描写对比">
    - What is particularly striking is that `[A]` `[does X]` while `[B]` `[does Y]`, which creates a sharp contrast.
    - The picture shows how `[person]` chooses to `[behavior]`, even though `[difficulty]` makes the situation challenging.
    - The contrast between `[A]` and `[B]` suggests that `[meaning]`, especially when `[condition]` is considered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="用 although / whose / by 强调细节">
    - Although the two pictures focus on the same theme, they present it from different angles, which makes the message clearer.
    - The key object, which is placed `[location]`, reinforces the idea that `[meaning]`.
    - By contrasting `[A]` and `[B]`, the picture draws attention to `[issue]`, which deserves careful reflection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 3. 第二段：分析原因或意义

### F3 分析链条

**1. 表达目的**

完成一条清晰论证。原因路径说明“什么条件如何影响行为，最终产生材料中的结果”；意义路径说明“某种品质或问题为什么重要，它带来什么影响”。

**2. 中文表达结构**

原因路径：`条件变化 → 人或机构的反应 → 材料中的现象`。

意义路径：`某种品质、行为或问题 → 现实影响 → 为什么值得重视`。

**3. 核心英文句型**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="原因路径">
    `条件变化 → 人或机构的反应 → 材料中的现象`

    > Because `[specific change]`, `[people or institutions]` are more likely to `[behavior]`. This helps explain `[the material phenomenon]`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="意义路径">
    `品质、行为或问题 → 现实影响 → 为什么值得重视`

    > `[Quality or behavior]` matters because it enables `[people]` to `[effect]`, especially when `[difficult condition]`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
题目强调“为什么会这样”时走原因路径；题目强调“这意味着什么”时走意义路径。图画寓意题通常优先考虑意义路径，不要硬套社会原因。
</Warning>

**4. 备用句型**

只有在确实需要说明风险时使用：

> This may also lead to `[specific risk]`.

**5. 填空说明**

- `[specific change]` 要写完整从句，例如 `household income grows`、`the population ages`、`appliances become more affordable`。
- `[behavior]` 要写成人的具体选择，例如购买、使用、提前规划、寻求帮助。
- `[the material phenomenon]` 必须回扣第一段，不能换题。
- 意义路径的 `[effect]` 要写成可观察结果，例如提高效率、减少压力、增强联系。
- `[difficult condition]` 写这种品质或行为真正发挥作用的场景。

**6. 使用条件与常见错误**

- 不要写 `Several factors may account for this phenomenon`，除非下一句真的列出因素。
- `be attributed to` 后接名词或动名词，不接完整句子。
- `contribute to` 表示“促成”，不是“归因于”。
- `give rise to` 表示“导致”，方向是原因到结果。
- 不要连续使用三个 `because`；可用 `one reason is that`、`this means that` 或 `especially when` 变化结构。
- 题目要求解释意义时，可以不写原因，直接走意义路径。

**7. 原因与意义句型扩展**

### 原因路径句型库

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Because 引导直接原因">
    - Because `[specific change]`, `[people]` are more likely to `[behavior]`.
    - Because `[resource]` has become more accessible, `[group]` can `[action]` more easily.
    - Because `[cost or risk]` remains high, many people are unwilling to `[action]`.

    用于因果关系明确、无需复杂铺垫的句子。不要把两个无关事实硬连成因果。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="As 引导背景变化">
    - As `[social or economic condition]` changes, `[people]` are increasingly likely to `[behavior]`.
    - As `[technology]` becomes more widely available, `[group]` are better able to `[action]`.

    `as` 有时可表示“因为”，有时可表示“随着”。如果因果关系是重点，优先使用 `Because`，避免歧义。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One reason / A key factor">
    - One reason is that `[condition]` has made `[behavior]` more `[necessary/feasible/attractive]`.
    - A key factor is `[factor]`, which encourages `[group]` to `[behavior]`.
    - Another factor is `[factor]`, which may help explain `[phenomenon]`.

    这种句型适合引出两到三个原因，但每个原因后面都要接作用机制。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="条件变化影响选择">
    - When `[condition]` improves, `[people]` are more likely to `[choice]`.
    - The more `[X]` becomes available, the more likely `[people]` are to `[Y]`.
    - If `[condition]` continues, `[phenomenon]` may become more common.

    `the more...the more...` 只适合两个变化之间有清楚关联的情况，不要滥用。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="进一步导致结果">
    - This change, in turn, leads to `[result]`.
    - As a result, `[phenomenon]` becomes more common.
    - This helps explain why `[material phenomenon]` appears.

    最后一句必须回扣第一段的数据、画面或文字材料，不能换成新话题。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="说明不是唯一原因">
    - This is not the only reason, but it helps explain `[phenomenon]`.
    - While other factors may also matter, `[factor]` plays an important role.
    - A combination of `[A]` and `[B]` may account for `[phenomenon]`.

    用于避免把复杂现象写成单一因果。两个因素必须有不同层次，不能只是同义重复。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="便利与成本机制">
    - `[Feature]` makes it easier/cheaper for `[group]` to `[action]`.
    - By reducing the time/cost required, `[X]` encourages `[group]` to `[action]`.
    - The convenience of `[X]` has made `[behavior]` more attractive.

    适合技术、服务、消费和生活方式类材料。`make it easier` 后接 `for somebody to do something`。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="风险与推广障碍">
    - High cost may discourage `[group]` from `[action]`.
    - Concerns about `[risk]` can prevent `[X]` from being widely adopted.
    - The lack of `[resource]` makes it difficult for `[group]` to `[action]`.

    可用于解释“接受度不高”“使用率有限”或“推广受阻”等现象。
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### 意义路径句型库

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="品质为什么重要">
    - `[Quality]` matters because it helps people `[effect]`.
    - `[Quality]` is valuable not only for `[short-term benefit]`, but also for `[long-term outcome]`.
    - Without `[quality]`, people may find it difficult to `[action]`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="行为改善现实结果">
    - `[Behavior]` enables people to `[effect]`.
    - By `[behavior]`, people can `[outcome]`.
    - `[Behavior]` allows `[group]` to `[do something]`.

    `enable` 强调“使之有能力”，`allow` 更偏“使之成为可能”，`help` 语气最弱。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="习惯影响长期表现">
    - Repeated `[habit]` can shape `[long-term outcome]`.
    - What people do regularly often determines how well they achieve `[goal]`.
    - Good habits make it easier for people to `[sustain result]`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="技术提高效率或便利性">
    - `[Technology]` can improve efficiency by `[mechanism]`.
    - `[Technology]` makes it easier for `[group]` to `[action]`.
    - `[Technology]` can reduce the time/cost needed to `[action]`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="社会行为促进沟通与合作">
    - `[Social behavior]` helps build trust and encourage cooperation.
    - `[Behavior]` makes communication more effective and strengthens social ties.
    - When people `[action]`, they are better able to address shared problems.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="问题为什么值得关注">
    - The issue deserves attention because it affects `[group]` in `[way]`.
    - Its significance lies not only in `[surface phenomenon]`, but in `[long-term consequence]`.
    - If the problem is ignored, `[negative outcome]` may become more likely.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="能力在困难条件下发挥作用">
    - `[Ability]` enables people to keep moving when `[difficulty occurs]`.
    - `[Quality]` helps people respond to `[challenge]` rather than give up.
    - In difficult situations, `[quality]` can make `[action]` possible.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="对个人、家庭和社会的意义">
    - For individuals, `[phenomenon]` can `[effect]`.
    - For families, it may `[effect]`.
    - At the social level, `[phenomenon]` can `[effect]`.

    不要机械写满个人、家庭和社会三个层面。只写材料真正涉及的层面。
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### 分析链条示例

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="条件变化 → 行为变化 → 结果">
    Because online services have become more accessible, more people are choosing to learn remotely. This helps explain the rise in digital education.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="个人习惯 → 短期影响 → 长期影响">
    Practicing regularly improves immediate performance. Over time, it builds confidence and self-discipline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="技术应用 → 现实收益 → 需要关注的风险">
    Automation increases efficiency, but it also raises concerns about privacy and responsibility.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="个人行动 → 群体效果 → 公共意义">
    When individuals reduce energy use, the collective effect can ease environmental pressure.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


**从句扩展：原因与意义**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="原因链条中的从句">
    - Because `[condition]` has changed, `[people]` are more likely to `[behavior]`, which helps explain `[phenomenon]`.
    - As `[resource]` becomes more widely available, `[group]` are better able to `[action]`, making `[phenomenon]` more common.
    - One reason is that `[condition]` has made `[behavior]` both easier and more necessary, especially for `[group]`.
    - Although `[factor]` cannot fully explain `[phenomenon]`, it plays an important role in shaping `[behavior]`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="意义链条中的从句">
    - The significance lies in the fact that `[quality]` enables people to `[effect]`, even when `[difficulty]` occurs.
    - What makes `[behavior]` important is that it can `[effect]` over time, which is why `[phenomenon]` deserves attention.
    - `[Quality]` is valuable not because it is easy, but because it helps people `[action]` when they face `[difficulty]`.
    - If this trend continues, it is likely that `[result]`, which would further affect `[group or society]`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 4. 第三段：评论与行动

第三段不是固定的“评价 + 建议”。按题目要求选择一条路径：

| 路径 | 使用功能 | 适用情况 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 只评价 | F4 | 题目只要求评论或表明态度 |
| 评价 + 建议 | F4 + F5 行动 | 题目要求评论并提出解决办法 |
| 只展望 | F5 展望 | 题目要求预测结果或未来趋势 |

<Warning>
F5 不是固定结尾。题目没有要求建议或未来时，写完 F4 就可以结束。
</Warning>

### F4 总体评论

**1. 表达目的**

在题目要求 `comment` 时给出明确立场。可以是积极、消极、谨慎或有条件，但必须来自前文分析。

**2. 中文表达结构**

`总体判断 + 核心理由`。需要辩证时，加一句限制或风险。

**3. 核心英文句型**

> In my view, this is `[evaluation]` mainly because `[core reason]`.

需要补充限制时：

> Although `[benefit]`, `[specific risk or condition]` should not be overlooked.

**4. 填空说明**

- `[evaluation]` 可用 `positive`、`concerning`、`a trade-off`、`beneficial under certain conditions`。
- `[core reason]` 必须来自 F3，不能引入新话题。
- `[risk or condition]` 要具体，例如成本、隐私、安全、执行能力。

**5. 使用条件与常见错误**

- 题目不要求 `comment` 时，可以不写 F4。
- 不要把 `Every coin has two sides` 当作默认 F4 句型；若使用，必须写出具体两面。
- 不要重复第一段数据。
- `double-edged` 只有确实同时有明显利弊时才用。

**6. 评论句型扩展**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="正面评价">
    - The trend is largely beneficial, especially because `[reason]`.
    - This development offers clear benefits in terms of `[efficiency/quality/access]`.
    - It is a positive step toward `[goal]`.
    - The change can improve `[area]` and make `[result]` more likely.
    - Such progress deserves recognition because it promotes `[value]`.

    适合政策、技术、教育、环保或个人成长的积极变化。理由要承接 F3。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="负面评价与风险">
    - This trend raises serious concerns, particularly regarding `[risk]`.
    - The development may create risks such as `[specific risk]`.
    - If left unmanaged, it could lead to `[negative outcome]`.
    - The potential costs may outweigh the benefits, especially when `[condition]`.
    - This practice may be harmful to `[group or value]` in the long run.

    适合成本、隐私、安全、环境或公平问题。不要只写 `bad` 或 `harmful`，后面必须说明具体后果。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="有条件的评价">
    - On the whole, this development is beneficial, provided that `[condition]`.
    - Its value depends on whether `[condition]` can be ensured.
    - This trend deserves support only if `[condition]` is met.
    - The benefits will be limited unless `[actor]` takes `[measure]`.
    - Whether the trend is positive depends largely on `[factor]`.

    适合总体认可但必须补充前提的题目，尤其是技术、消费和公共政策类材料。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="辩证评价">
    - The trend brings both opportunities and risks.
    - Its benefits are real, but they come with `[specific cost]`.
    - The same feature that produces convenience may also create `[risk]`.
    - Whether it does more good than harm depends on `[condition]`.
    - The development should be judged by both its short-term effects and long-term consequences.

    只有确实同时存在明显利弊时才使用，不要机械写“利弊并存”。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="价值判断">
    - What matters most is not `[surface factor]`, but `[underlying value]`.
    - The real value lies in `[quality/behavior/change]`.
    - This behavior deserves recognition because it promotes `[value]`.
    - The issue should be judged by its long-term effect on `[group or society]`.
    - Such a change is meaningful because it promotes `[value]`.

    适合结尾总结，不要再引入新的论据或新话题。
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### F5 行动或展望

**1. 表达目的**

题目要求解决、促进或预测未来时，写出具体主体、措施和结果；题目不要求时直接删除。

**2. 中文表达结构**

行动路径：`主体 + 措施 + 预期结果`。

展望路径：`条件 + 可能结果`。

**3. 核心英文句型**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="行动路径">
    主体 + 措施 + 预期结果：

    > `[Specific actor]` should `[measure]`, so that `[result]`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="展望路径">
    条件 + 可能结果：

    > If `[condition]`, `[positive outcome]` is more likely.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**4. 填空说明**

- 政府：`provide subsidies`、`set safety standards`、`improve regulation`。
- 企业：`lower costs`、`improve safety`、`make products more accessible`。
- 学校：`provide guidance`、`create supportive environments`。
- 个人：`plan ahead`、`seek help`、`develop a habit`。
- 行动主体和措施必须匹配。

**5. 使用条件与常见错误**

- 题目只要求评论时，删除 F5。
- 不要写 `government, schools and individuals should work together`，除非三者都有实际作用。
- 不要用 `Only in this way`。
- 不要把 F5 写成新的论点。

**6. 行动与展望句型扩展**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="个人行动">
    - Individuals should take the initiative to `[action]`.
    - People can start by `[small action]`.
    - It is advisable for individuals to `[action]`.
    - Everyone can contribute to `[goal]` by `[specific behavior]`.

    适合习惯、学习、环保、健康和个人成长类主题。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="学校行动">
    - Schools can integrate `[topic]` into `[courses or activities]`.
    - Schools should provide `[guidance or resources]` for students.
    - Universities can create a supportive environment for `[behavior]`.
    - Teachers can help students develop `[ability or habit]`.

    适合教育、阅读、创新、心理健康和价值观培养类主题。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="企业行动">
    - Companies should invest in `[technology or training]`.
    - Firms can improve `[product or service]` by `[method]`.
    - Businesses should make `[product or service]` more affordable/accessible.

## 5. 语言准确性工具箱

高级词只保留下面这些高频搭配。先保证准确，再考虑升级。

| 功能 | 固定搭配 | 注意 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占比例 | `account for X%` | 不接数量本身 |
| 趋势 | `rise/fall from X to Y` | 必须写起点和终点 |
| 稳定 | `remain relatively stable` | 可接 `at around X`，但数据必须真的稳定 |
| 比较 | `be higher/lower than that of...` | 比较对象范围一致 |
| 可能 | `be more likely to` | 比绝对因果更稳 |
| 因果 | `help explain`、`lead to` | 回扣材料现象 |
| 作用 | `enable...to...`、`make it possible for...to...` | 后面写具体效果 |
| 评论 | `should not be overlooked` | 用于具体风险或条件 |

### 5.1 最容易失分的地方

1. `number of` 接可数名词，谓语通常用单数；`amount of` 接不可数名词。
2. `rise` 是不及物动词，`raise` 是及物动词。
3. `because` 后接句子，`because of` 后接名词或动名词。
4. `although` 后接句子，`despite` 后接名词或动名词。
5. `account for` 接比例或原因，`contribute to` 表示促成，两者语义不同。
6. `compared with` 表示“与……相比”，`compare A to B` 常表示“把 A 比作 B”。
7. 百分比增加说 `rise by X percentage points`，不同比例的对比说 `compared with X%`。
8. `data` 在正式写作中常视为复数，但为避免争议，优先使用 `the figures show`。

### 5.2 不要默认使用的表达

- `With the rapid development of society`
- `Every coin has two sides`
- `Only in this way can we`
- `It is universally acknowledged that`
- `More and more people`
- `play an increasingly important role`
- `double-edged sword`

> 如果一句话删掉后不影响论证，它大概率就是空话。

### 8.1 2019 英语一：登山与坚持

**材料事实**

两个人爬山。一个人坐下说“太累了，不爬了”，另一个人递水并说“休息一下接着爬”。图片下方文字为“旅途”。

来源：[新东方解析](https://kaoyan.xdf.cn/201812/10837501.html)。

**选择功能**：F1 + F2 + F3 + F4；F5 为可选行动或展望。

**英文示范**

The picture shows two climbers on a mountain journey. One sits down and says he is too tired to continue, while the other hands him water and encourages him to rest before moving on. The key contrast is between giving up and continuing. This contrast suggests that perseverance matters when people face difficulty.

Perseverance matters because it enables people to keep making progress when motivation declines, especially when a goal takes a long time to achieve. The second climber's action also suggests that support and a short rest can help someone continue rather than quit.

In my view, this is a valuable lesson. Rather than forcing ourselves to struggle alone, we should set realistic sub-goals and seek support when necessary, so that we can maintain momentum and continue toward our goals.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### 8.3 图画类总结

图画类固定使用 F1 画面描述、F2 关键细节或对比；F3 优先写意义路径，必要时再解释原因；F4 评论，必要时 F5 行动或展望。不要为了套模板强行加入社会原因，也不要把画面中的普通动作无限拔高。

## 9. 最终背诵版

### 9.1 五个中文功能

1. **F1 定位材料**：材料是什么，讨论谁、什么时间或什么场景。
2. **F2 提取重点并概括**：最关键的数据或细节是什么，它说明什么。
3. **F3 分析原因或意义**：为什么发生，或者为什么重要。
4. **F4 总体评论**：我的总体判断是什么。
5. **F5 行动或展望**：需要时写具体措施或条件结果。

F1 通常必写；F2 由 `describe` 决定；F3 由 `interpret` 决定；F4 由 `comment` 决定；F5 只在题目需要建议或未来时写。谚语不在必背骨架内；需要时放在 F3 末尾或 F4，最多一句。

### 9.2 图画类骨架

单幅图：

> The picture shows how `[person]` chooses to `[behavior]` in `[context]`. What is most striking is that `[detail]`, which suggests `[meaning]`.

双幅图：

> While the first picture shows `[first scene]`, the second shows `[second scene]`, which creates a sharp contrast. The key contrast between `[A]` and `[B]` suggests that `[meaning]`.

> `[Quality or behavior]` matters because it enables `[people]` to `[effect]`, especially when `[difficult condition]`.

> In my view, this is `[evaluation]`. `[Actor]` should `[measure]`, so that `[result]`.

### 9.3 行动或展望二选一

需要具体措施：

> `[Specific actor]` should `[measure]`, so that `[result]`.

只需要未来展望：

> If `[condition]`, `[positive outcome]` is more likely.

如果题目没有要求建议或展望，直接删除 F5。

### 9.4 考场审题表

| 题目信号 | 处理 |
| --- | --- |
| `describe the chart/picture` | 写 F1 + F2 |
| `interpret the chart/picture` | 写 F3 |
| `give your comments` | 写 F4 |
| 要求措施、建议、未来 | 写 F5 |
| 只要求描述 | 不写 F3、F4、F5 |
| 没有明确原因 | 走意义路径，不编原因 |
| 没有时间写长文 | 至少保留 F1 + F2 + F3 + F4 |

### 9.5 语言检查

- 单图 `chart`，多图 `charts`。
- `account for` 后接比例或原因。
- `number of` 接可数名词，`amount of` 接不可数名词。
- `rise` 不接宾语，`raise` 接宾语。
- `because` 后接句子，`because of` 后接名词或动名词。
- `although` 后接句子，`despite` 后接名词或动名词。
- 趋势句写清 `from X to Y`。
- 比较对象保持一致。
- 用 `This pattern/trend/contrast/detail` 明确指代上一句，再写规律或含义。
- 不用没有把握的谚语和高级词。

### 9.6 内容检查

- 第一段是否写清材料对象和范围。
- 图表是否只保留一个主关系。
- 图画是否写清关键细节或对比。
- 第二段是否形成“条件 → 行为 → 结果”或“品质 → 影响 → 意义”的链条。
- 第三段是否只写题目要求的内容。
- 是否误用了“政府、学校、个人共同努力”。
- 删除 F5 后文章是否仍然完整。
- 是否有空泛谚语、空泛原因或空泛措施。

### 9.7 最后的写作顺序

1. 读题，圈出 `describe`、`interpret`、`comment`。
2. 判断图表、图画还是文字材料。
3. 用中文写下 F1 到 F4，必要时加 F5。
4. 先填材料和数据，再填原因或意义，最后填评论或行动。
5. 用核心句型连成英文。
6. 检查语法、搭配、数据和逻辑。

> **最终原则**：模板只负责组织思路，不负责替考生思考。写清材料事实，建立一条完整论证链，比背更多句型、谚语和高级词更重要。
