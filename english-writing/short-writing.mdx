<Info>
小作文的核心不是复杂句，而是**体裁正确、目的明确、要点齐全、语气得体**。先判断是书信、邮件还是通知，再按题目逐条落实内容。
</Info>

## 1. 通用结构

1. 称呼：`Dear Professor Smith,` / `Dear Paul,` / `Dear Sir or Madam,`
2. 开头：说明写信目的。`I am writing to...`
3. 主体：按要求提供信息、解释原因、提出请求或表达感谢。
4. 收尾：期待回复、感谢帮助或表达祝愿。
5. 落款：`Yours sincerely,` / `Best regards,` / `Li Ming`

<Warning>
不要把大作文的议论结构搬进小作文。小作文优先写清任务，不追求观点深度。
</Warning>

## 2. 常见体裁与句型

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="书信 / 邮件：目的、请求与回应">
    - `I am writing to express my interest in...`
    - `I am writing to ask for more information about...`
    - `I would like to invite you to...`
    - `Could you please tell me whether...`
    - `I would appreciate it if you could...`
    - `I am sorry to inform you that...`
    - `Thank you for your help and consideration.`
    - `I look forward to your reply.`

    **检查**：称呼、落款、语气和收信人身份要匹配。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="建议信：提出建议与理由">
    - `I would like to suggest that...`
    - `It might be helpful to...`
    - `One practical solution is to...`
    - `Another suggestion is that...`
    - `I hope you will find these suggestions useful.`

    **检查**：每条建议都要具体，不能只写 `should improve`。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="邀请信：时间、地点与活动安排">
    - `I am writing to invite you to...`
    - `The event will be held in... on...`
    - `It will start at... and last for...`
    - `We would be delighted if you could join us.`
    - `Please let me know whether you are available.`

    **检查**：时间、地点、活动名称不要遗漏。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="感谢信：感谢原因与具体帮助">
    - `I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for...`
    - `Your help meant a great deal to me.`
    - `Without your support, I would not have...`
    - `Thank you again for your kindness and patience.`

    **检查**：感谢要具体到对方做了什么。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="道歉信：说明问题与补救措施">
    - `I am writing to apologize for...`
    - `I am sorry that I failed to...`
    - `Please accept my sincere apology for...`
    - `To make up for my mistake, I will...`
    - `I hope you can understand my situation.`

    **检查**：先承认问题，再提出补救办法。
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="通知：活动信息与参与方式">
    - `We are pleased to inform you that...`
    - `The activity is scheduled to take place on...`
    - `Those who are interested are welcome to...`
    - `Please sign up before...`
    - `For further information, please contact...`

    **检查**：通知通常需要标题、时间、地点、对象、活动内容和报名方式。
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 3. 最后检查

- 体裁是否正确：书信、邮件还是通知？
- 称呼和落款是否完整？
- 题目要求的要点是否全部出现？
- 语气是否礼貌、自然？
- 时间、地点、人物和事件是否前后一致？
- 是否写成了大作文式议论？
