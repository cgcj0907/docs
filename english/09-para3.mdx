## 3.3 通用的第三段：总结全文+具体措施+畅想未来

### 第八句：总结全文

总结全文即重申中心思想，第三句中的方案也可使用。句子开头需加：All in all/ To sum up / In general/ Generally speaking/ On the whole。

**方案一：从句（与第二段中的方案三类似）**

**公式 1：**It is not difficult to draw the conclusion that enough attention must be paid

to 主题词 by ourselves and our community.

**例 2024 英一图画+图表（公园建设）：**

It is not difficult to draw the conclusion that enough attention has been paid to the infrastructure by the departments concerned.

不难得出结论，有关部门对基础设施给予了足够的重视。 （也可以得出居民的生活环境越来越好的结论）（注意根据情况变换时态）

**例 2024 英二柱状图（劳动实践）：**

It is not difficult to draw the conclusion that enough attention must be paid to the practical courses by ourselves and our universities.

不难得出结论，我们个人和大学都必须对实践课程给予足够的重视。

**公式 2：**We should bear in mind that 主题词 is of great importance to 某方面.

**注：**参考六大方面，主要从个人、行业、社会的角度出发考虑，例如：our personal development 我们个人发展、both our daily lives and studies 我们的生活和学习、 industry development 行业发展、social progress 社会进步

**例 2024 英二柱状图（劳动实践）：**

We should bear in mind that practical courses are of great importance to our personal development.我们应牢记在心的是社会实践对我们个人发展很重要。

**例 2019 英一图画（坚持）：**

We should bear in mind that the spirit of perseverance is of great importance to both our daily lives and studies.

我们应牢记在心的是坚持的精神对我们的生活和学习很重要。

**例 2026 英二柱状图（儿童户外活动）：**

We should bear in mind that outdoor activities are indispensable to children's physical and mental development.

我们应当牢记，户外活动对儿童的身心发展不可或缺。

**注：**本方案特别适用于意志品质类的作文，例如 the spirit of perseverance/never giving up/lifelong learning/selfless dedication/cooperation 坚持/永不放弃/终身学习/无私奉献/团结协作的精神

**公式 3：**The discussion leads us to the realization that 主题词 is not an option, but

a necessity.

**例 2021 英一图画（文化传承或坚持）、2023 英一图画（文化传承）：**

The discussion leads us to the realization that cultural inheritance is not an option, but a necessity.

讨论使我们认识到，文化传承不是一种选择，而是一种必要。把 inheritance 换成 exchange 适用于 2002（美国小女孩）、2010 英一图画（文化火锅）。

**例 2025 英一表格（家电）、2025 英二柱状图（老年人休闲活动）：**

The discussion leads us to the realization that a high-quality lifestyle is not an option, but a necessity.

讨论使我们认识到，高质量生活方式不是一种选择，而是一种必要。把主题词换成 healthy 适用 2023 英二线状图（居民健康素养水平）。

**例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人）：**

The discussion leads us to the realization that elderly care is not a luxury, but a must for an aging society.

讨论使我们认识到，养老不是奢侈品，而是老龄化社会的必需品。

**公式 4：**We are faced with the undeniable fact that 主题词 has become

an integral part of our lives.

increasingly accessible to the general public.

例 2009 英一图画（网络）、2015 英一图画（手机聚会）、2020 英二饼状图（手机阅读） ：

We are faced with the undeniable fact that the Internet has become an integral part of our lives.我们面临着一个不可否认的事实，即互联网已经成为我们生活中不可或缺的一部分。

**例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人）：**

We are faced with the undeniable fact that elderly-care robots have become increasingly popular with the general public.

我们面临着一个不可否认的事实，养老机器人已经越来越受公众欢迎。

We are faced with the undeniable fact that the majority of consumers accept elderly-care robots, with safety as their primary concern.

我们面临着一个不可否认的事实，大多数消费者接受养老机器人，安全是他们的首要关注点。（大改）

**例 2026 英二柱状图（儿童户外活动）：**

We are faced with the undeniable fact that outdoor activities have become an integral part of children's growth.

我们面临着一个不可否认的事实，户外活动已经成为儿童成长中不可或缺的一部分。

**方案二：**否定词（组）倒装

结构：否定词+助动词+主谓宾。看不懂没关系，记住下方组合公式，考场上只需要写出与主题相关的动词短语。

**公式：**

| Never can we<br />On no account should we<br />Under no circumstances can we<br />By no means should we | ✖ | give up our efforts to...<br />underestimate the profound impact of...<br />cease our pursuit of...<br />doubt the importance of...<br />... |
|---|---|---|

**例 2011 英一图画（环保）：**

By no means should we give up our efforts to protect the environment.

我们决不能放弃在保护环境上的努力。

**例 2025 英一表格（家电）：**

Under no circumstances should we cease our pursuit of a more convenient and intelligent lifestyle.

在任何情况下，我们都不应停止对更便捷、更智能生活方式的追求。

**例 2025 英二柱状图（老人休闲）：**

Never shall we give up our efforts to ensure the well-being of senior citizens.

我们永远不会放弃为确保老年人福祉而努力。

**例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人）：**

Under no circumstances should we ignore consumers' safety concerns when developing elderly-care robots.

在任何情况下，我们在开发养老机器人时都不应忽视消费者的安全关切。

**例 2026 英二柱状图（儿童户外活动）：**

By no means should we underestimate the profound impact of outdoor activities on children's curiosity and observational skills.

我们决不能低估户外活动对儿童好奇心和观察力的深远影响。

**方案三：**万能但不够针对

It is evident that the trend/ distribution reflected in the chart deserves our careful consideration.

很明显，图表反映的趋势/分布值得我们认真考虑。

There is no denying that further attention must be paid to the scene/phenomenon/situation/tendency mentioned above.

不可否认，对上述场景/现象/情况/趋势必须给予进一步的关注。

Given the above analysis, it is not difficult to understand why this phenomenon has aroused widespread concern.

鉴于以上分析，不难理解为什么这一现象引起了广泛关注。

**注：**万能且无需修改任何内容，应急使用。

### 第九、十句：具体措施

**方案一：**一句一个角度，两句并列

五个角度：政府作为、公民意识、个人行为、媒体呼吁、家庭教育。任选三个角度掌握，肯定可以命中两个，可单独使用也可用连接词相连，以 2011 年“保护环境”为例，直接背例句。

政府作为：It is imperative for the authorities to make strict laws to protect the environment.当局必须制定严格的法律来保护环境。

**make strict laws** 制定法律 → **formulate policies** 制定政策 / **allocate more funds** 拨付更多资金 / **launch public initiatives** 发起公共倡议

公民意识：We should also raise environmental awareness among children and adults. 我们还应该增强孩子和大人的环境保护意识。

It is high time that we raised public consciousness of environmental protection.

是时候增强公众环保意识了。

个人行为：We should spare no pains to protect the environment and make the"global village" better. 我们应该不遗余力地保护环境，让“地球村”变得更好。

**注 1：**We should 可替换为 It is our duty to / We are obliged to / We ought to

**注 2：**spare no pains to 可替换为 make concerted efforts to /take concrete steps to

媒体呼吁：New media platforms, ranging from short video apps to social media, should appeal to users to adopt a sustainable lifestyle.

新媒体平台，从短视频应用到社交媒体，应该呼吁用户采取可持续的生活方式。

Social media platforms should play a pivotal role in raising public awareness of sustainable living.

社交媒体平台应该在增强公众可持续生活意识方面发挥关键作用。

家庭教育：Parents and teachers should set a good example for children in resource conservation through their own behavior.

家长和老师应当通过规范自身为孩子们树立节约能源的榜样。

Parents can serve as role models for their children by saving resources in daily lives.

家长可以通过日常生活中节约资源，为孩子树立榜样。

**例 2025 英一表格（家电）：**

For one thing, it is imperative for the authorities to formulate industry-friendly policies to promote the development of the home appliance industry. For another, enterprises ought to spare no pains to enhance product quality and reduce commodity prices.

一方面，当局必须制定友好政策来促进家电行业的发展。另一方面，企业应该不遗余力地提高产品质量，降低商品价格。

**例 2025 英二柱状图（老人休闲）：**

For one thing, it is imperative for the authorities to allocate more funds to build more elderly activity centers. For another, new media platforms should call on people to care for the elderly.一方面，当局必须投入更多资金建设更多老年活动中心。

另一方面，新媒体平台应该呼吁人们关爱老人。

**例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人）：**

For one thing, it is imperative for the authorities to formulate strict regulations to ensure the safety of elderly-care robots. For another, enterprises ought to spare no pains to improve product quality.

一方面，当局必须制定严格法规确保养老机器人的安全。另一方面，企业应该不遗余力地提高产品质量。

**例 2026 英二柱状图（儿童户外活动）：**

For one thing, it is imperative for the authorities to improve public facilities to provide a safer and more friendly environment for children's outdoor activities.

For another, parents and schools should create more opportunities for children to participate in outdoor activities.

一方面，当局必须完善公共设施，为儿童户外活动提供更安全、更友好的环境。

另一方面，家长和学校应该为儿童创造更多参与户外活动的机会。

领会思想，灵活运用！

**方案二：**老子说的才是对的

第一句抛出大多数人的做法，转折词相连，第二句给出自己的看法，以彰显自己的独到见解。

**待选 1：**When it comes to concrete solutions, some people hold that A must take

responsibility for B. In fact, it is C that plays a crucial role in D.

**注释：**

A——其他人认为的措施的实施方 the government department concerned（有关部门）

B——主题词以环境保护为例：the protection of the environment C——你认为的措施的实施方 cultivating citizens’ consciousness（培养公民意识）

D——主题词，但不要和 B 一模一样

以环境保护为例的最终例句：

When it comes to concrete solutions, some people hold that the government department concerned must take responsibility for the protection of the environment. In fact, it is cultivating citizens’ consciousness that plays a crucial role in protecting the environment.

当涉及具体的解决方案时，一些人认为有关部门必须承担起保护环境的责任。

事实上，培养公民意识在保护环境中起着至关重要的作用。

**例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人）：**

When it comes to concrete solutions, some people hold that the government must take responsibility for regulating the elderly-care robot industry. In fact, it is technological advancement and strict safety standards that play a pivotal role in gaining public trust.

谈到具体解决方案，一些人认为政府必须承担监管养老机器人行业的责任。事实上，技术进步和严格的安全标准在获得公众信任中起到关键作用。

**待选 2：**On the surface, A seems to be a sound solution, but on careful consideration,

we find that B is a more reasonable and effective approach.

**注释：**

A——解决措施 1 Setting rules based on scenic location（景区制定规则）

B——解决措施 2 raising public awareness / promoting civic awareness（改善公民意识）

以环境保护为例的最终例句：

On the surface, setting rules based on scenic location seems to be a sound solution, but on careful consideration, we find that raising public awareness is a more reasonable and effective approach.

表面上，根据景区位置来制定规则似乎是一个合理的解决方案，但经过仔细考虑，我们会发现增强公众意识才是更合理、更有效的方法。

**例 2025 英二柱状图（老人休闲）：**

On the surface, building more elderly activity centers seems to be a sound solution, but on careful consideration, we find that family companionship is a more reasonable and effective approach.

表面上，建设更多老年活动中心似乎是一个合理的解决方案，但仔细考虑后，我们发现家庭陪伴才是更合理、更有效的方法。

**注：**其实 26 考研英一英二都不太适合方案二，原因是写出来字数太多了。方案二适用于主题词、实施方、解决措施较短的题目。

### 第十一句：畅想未来

均为万能句，任选 2 句背住。

**待选 1：** If these proposed measures are implemented, a harmonious balance between

A and B will be within our reach.（注：A、B 根据主题词来定）

如果这些提议的措施得以实施，A 和 B 之间的平衡触手可及。

**待选 2：** Only through the joint efforts of all people can we effectively solve the

problem/ have a better life/ advance social progress. 只有通过所有人的共同努力，我们才能有效地解决问题/拥有更好的生活/推动社会进步。

**待选 3 ：** In this way, hopefully, we may embrace a brighter and more promising

future. 这样我们才有希望拥抱一个更光明、更有希望的未来。

**待选 4：**There is no better time than now for us to take collective and decisive action

to address this pressing issue.

现在是我们采取集体和果断行动解决这一紧迫问题的最佳时机。

**例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人）：**

Only through the joint efforts of the government, enterprises and the public can we effectively promote the healthy development of elderly-care robots.

只有通过政府、企业和公众的共同努力，我们才能有效促进养老机器人的健康发展。

**例 2026 英二柱状图（儿童户外活动）：**

If these proposed measures are implemented, a harmonious balance between children's physical exercise and intellectual development will be within our reach.

如果这些提议的措施得以实施，儿童体育锻炼与智力发展之间的和谐平衡触手可及。

**注 1：**待选 1 和 2 需小改以适配题目；待选 3 和 4 不需要改，但很明显是套话，用于不会修改 1 和 2、时间紧张和字数不够的情况。

至此，第三段总结全文+具体措施+畅想未来讲解完毕。同时，你也在不知不觉中完成了 25 和 26 年英一英二真题套用。考虑到第三段是图画作文图表作文通用的，接下来以 2024 年英一图画图表结合（公园/基础设施建设）为例，完整的第三段是：

It is not difficult to draw the conclusion that enough attention has been paid to the infrastructure by the departments concerned. For one thing, it is imperative for the authorities to increase investment continuously. For another, new media platforms, ranging from short video apps to social media, should appeal to people to live a healthy life. In this way, hopefully, we may embrace a brighter and more promising future.

不难得出结论，有关部门已对基础设施给予足够的重视。一方面，当局必须不断增加投资。另一方面，新媒体平台，从短视频应用到社交媒体，应该呼吁人们过健康的生活。通过这种方式，希望我们能拥抱一个更光明、更有希望的未来。

### 图表作文第三段补充写法

图表作文第三段还可以：总结全文+趋势预测，这是最简单的写法。

**待选 1：**

Taking into account all the factors discussed above, we may reasonably conclude that, with the passage of time and the advancement of society, the number of people who choose to do Sth. will continue to increase/decline in the coming years.

综合考虑上述所有因素，我们可以合理地得出结论：随着时代变迁和社会进步，未来几年选择做某事的人数将持续增加/减少。

**待选 2：**

For the reasons given above, we can predict that an increasing number of people will focus on 主题词/choose to do Sth, and this trend is likely to continue in the near future.

基于上述原因，我们可以预测，将有越来越多的人关注主题词/选择做某事，这一趋势在不久的将来很可能会持续下去。

**待选 3：**

Given the arguments above, the next few years will also witness larger-scale, faster-paced, and higher-quality development in 主题词.

鉴于上述论点，未来几年主题词也将迎来更大规模、更快速度和更高水平发展。

**待选 4：**

In conclusion, the phenomenon reflected in the chart is bound to continue, and it will keep exerting a positive influence on our lives.

总之，图表所反映的现象必将持续下去，并将继续对我们的生活带来积极影响。

**待选 5：**

In a word, as society keeps moving forward, 主题词 is likely to gain even greater significance in the days to come.

总之，随着社会不断前进，主题词在未来很可能变得更加重要。

例 2025 英一表格（家电，动态）

Taking into account all the factors discussed above, we may reasonably conclude that, with the passage of time and the advancement of society, the number of families who own smart home appliances will continue to increase in the coming years.

综合考虑上述所有因素，我们可以合理地得出结论：随着时代变迁和社会进步，未来几年拥有智能家电的家庭数量将持续增加。

例 2025 英二柱状图（老人休闲，静态）

In conclusion, the phenomenon reflected in the chart is bound to continue, and it will keep exerting a positive influence on the well-being of the elderly.

总之，图表所反映的现象必将持续下去，并将继续对老年人的福祉带来积极影响。

例 2026 英一饼图+柱状图（养老机器人，静态）

In a word, as society keeps moving forward, elderly-care robots are likely to gain even greater significance in the days to come.

总之，随着社会不断前进，养老机器人在未来很可能变得更加重要。

例 2026 英二柱状图（儿童户外活动，静态）

In conclusion, the phenomenon reflected in the chart is bound to continue, and it will keep exerting a positive influence on children's healthy growth.

总之，图表所反映的现象必将持续下去，并将继续对儿童的健康成长带来积极影响。（略显生硬）

Given the arguments above, we can predict that outdoor activities will win growing recognition among parents as they increasingly value children's all-round development.

鉴于上述论点，随着家长愈发重视孩子的全面发展，我们可以预测户外活动将赢得越来越多家长的认可。（总结成待选 6：愈发重视，获得认可）

**注：**趋势预测的深度不及建议措施，仅应急使用。

至此，图表作文讲解完毕。
