## 第二段真题带练

**例 2022 年英语一写给英国大学教授的邀请信：**

51. Directions:

Write an email to a professor at a British university, inviting him/her to organize a team for the international innovation contest to be held at your university. You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Li Ming" instead. Do not write the address. (10 points)

Firstly, the contest will be held on the playground in my university at 3:00 p.m. on Sunday, December 31st. Moreover, a series of competitions such as robot competition and aircraft competition will be held to arouse students' enthusiasm for innovation. In the end, the experience will be memorable and our prize will be very valuable.

首先，比赛将于 12 月 31 日(周日)下午 3 点在我的大学操场举行。此外，将举办机器人比赛、飞机比赛等一系列竞赛项目，以激发学生的创新热情。最后，这次经历将是难忘的，我们的奖品将是非常有价值的。

**例 2022 年英语二写给留学生的美食节邀请信：**

47.Directions:

Suppose you are planning a campus food festival. Write an email to the international students in your university.

1) introduce the food festival; 2) invite them to participate.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET 2.Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Zhang Wei" instead. Do not write the address.

(10 points)

Firstly, the food festival will be held on the playground in my university at 5:00 p.m.

on Sunday, December 31st. Moreover, a series of interesting activities such as cooking contests and food shows will be held to arouse students' enthusiasm. In the end, the experience will be memorable and our food will be very delicious.

首先，美食节将于 12 月 31 日(周日)晚上 5 点在我校操场上举行。此外，还将举办一系列有趣的活动，如烹饪比赛和美食表演，以激发学生的热情。最后，这次经历将是难忘的，我们的食物将是非常美味的。

**或者**：Firstly, the food festival is scheduled to be held on the playground on Friday from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. Moreover, the food festival allows you to experience different food cultures and social customs. In the end, students of all grades are eligible to take part in the festival, including both domestic and international students.

首先，美食节计划于周五晚上五点到七点在操场举行。此外，美食节能让你接触不同美食文化和社会风俗。最后，所有年级的学生都有资格参加美食节，包括国内和国际学生。

**例 2019 年英语一关于援助乡村小学的答复信：**

**例 2007 年写给图书馆的建议信（投诉信）：**

![图](/images/english/s-p024-2.jpg)

In the first place, we are badly in need of updating the database, which can help us acquire the latest knowledge. In the second place, due to the noisy environment, it’s impossible for us to study effectively. In the third place, I wonder if the library can extend the opening hours so that we can have more hours to study.

首先，我们迫切需要更新数据库，它可以帮助我们获得最新的知识。第二，由于环境嘈杂，我们不可能有效地学习。第三，我想知道图书馆是否可以延长开放时间，这样我们就可以有更多的时间学习。

请大家开动脑筋，往六大方面去靠，再写三点建议。

例 2024 年英语一（答复信，实质是建议信、推荐信）

![图](/images/english/s-p024-1.jpg)

In the first place, you are badly in need of doing some research to gather detailed information, which can help you learn about their life, achievements, and contributions. Additionally, you could have more opportunities to catch your audience's attention if you include some interesting stories or examples related to the scientist. In the third place, I wonder if you can share your draft with me so that I can offer you some specific advice.

首先，你非常需要做一些研究来收集详细信息，这能帮助你了解他们的生活、成就和贡献。其次，如果你能包含一些与这位科学家有关的有趣故事或例子，你会有更多机会吸引听众的注意力。第三，我不知道你是否能把你的草稿发给我，这样我就能给你提供一些具体的建议。

**例 2024 年英语二（建议信）**

例 2025 年英语一（答复信，实质是介绍信、邀请信）

The innovative craft-making exhibition will be held in the student activity center at 8:00 a.m. on Sunday, December 20th. The exhibition will feature a keynote speech on craft-making, innovative crafts from all over the world, and a hands-on session. I wonder if you can arrive at the exhibition area by 7:00 a.m. so that we can assist with the final preparations together.

创意手工艺展将于 12 月 20 日（周日）上午 8:00 在学生活动中心举行。本次展览将以手工制作为主题演讲、世界各地的创新工艺、实践环节为特色。不知您能否在上午 7 点前到达展区，以便我们一起协助最后的准备工作。

例 2025 年英语二（介绍信、邀请信）

The short play is adapted from Journey to the West, one of China's Four Great Classical Novels. Given your energy and lively personality, you are the perfect choice for the role of the Monkey King. This play offers a unique opportunity for us to explore creativity together. Rehearsals will be held on weekends at the student activity center, starting next month.

这部短剧改编自中国四大古典名著之一《西游记》。鉴于你充沛的精力和活泼的性格，你非常适合出演美猴王这一角色。本剧将为我们提供一次共同探索创意的独特机会。排练将于下个月起在学生活动中心的周末举行。

例 2026 年英语一（答复信，实质是介绍信，介绍展览，邀请参加）

Firstly, everyone agrees that the family letter is key to passing down family values and love from generation to generation. Moreover, these letters provide a unique opportunity to understand the wisdom and expectations of our elders. Additionally, the family letter exhibition will be held in the student activity center next Saturday.

In the end, I wonder if you can come to the exhibition so that we can enjoy it together.

首先，大家都认为，家书是家风与亲情代代相传的关键。此外，这些家书提供了一个独特的机会，让我们理解长辈的智慧与期望。另外，家书展览将于下周六在学生活动中心举行。最后，我想知道你是否能来参观这个展览，以便我们一起欣赏。

难点是想到家书带来的“好处”，可参照改写自大作文模板中的六大方面：

Firstly, the family letter helps us inherit and carry forward our family traditions.

Moreover, these letters provide a unique opportunity to understand our elders' love and expectations. 首先，家书帮助我们继承和弘扬家族传统。此外，这些家书提供了一个独特的机会，让我们理解长辈的爱与期望。

例 2026 年英语二（答复信，表达感受，了解更多）

Firstly, the video allows me to experience the happiness of your family trip.

Moreover, it shows not only the beautiful scenery, but also the happy smiles of your family. In the end, I wonder if you can tell me more about your trip so that I can visit the same places with my parents one day.

首先，这段视频让我体验到你们家庭旅行的快乐。此外，它展示了美丽的风景，还有你们家人开心的笑容。最后，我想知道你是否能告诉我更多旅行的情况，以便我有一天也能带着父母去同样的地方。

注意学习上述例子是如何灵活运用这些有限的句型的，建议将我给出的常用句挑出 20 个左右，背会并练习灵活套用。在此基础上，学着我的方式，从背过的作文和大作文模板中选出一些稍微万能一些的句子作为本讲义的补充，能者多劳。同时，上方这部分内容也是通知、告示、纪要的核心内容，他们只是格式不同而已。对于一封邀请信，如果将邀请对象换成全体学生，那么这就是告示。例如 2022 年英语二，题干中明确说到是写 Email，所以这是书信，如果题干没提，那该年份也可以写成通知、告示的形式。
